01

What we found

Sharpflow already has what most consultancies are missing before a paid launch: a Diamond tier ClickUp partner listing, enterprise client logos and a clear discovery call offer. The risk is not the offer. It is that Google will happily spend your budget on ClickUp users who will never buy consulting unless the account is built to filter them out from day one.

Most "ClickUp" searches come from DIY users, not buyers

High impact

ClickUp is a large consumer and SMB product, so the bulk of search volume around its name is support, login, pricing and template intent. Broad or loosely matched keywords will fill the account with clicks from people trying to use the tool, not hire someone to build it. Exact and phrase match, plus a negative list built before launch, decide whether the budget reaches a COO or a freelancer.

Block before launch

  • clickup login, clickup app download
  • clickup free plan, clickup pricing
  • clickup templates, clickup tutorial
  • clickup jobs, clickup certification
  • clickup alternatives, clickup vs notion (DIY research)

Target from day one

  • clickup consultant, clickup implementation partner
  • clickup implementation services, clickup onboarding services
  • migrate asana to clickup, monday to clickup migration
  • jira to clickup migration for teams
  • clickup enterprise setup, clickup workflow automation consultant

The number that matters sits outside Google Ads today

High impact

Google Tag Manager is already installed on sharpflowconsulting.com, so tracking can be added without touching the site build. The gap is further down the funnel. A booked discovery call is not a qualified opportunity, and if Google only ever sees form fills, it will optimise toward whoever fills forms most cheaply. The fix is sending the qualified call stage from your CRM back into Google Ads.

1

Week 1: Tracking and qualification first

Add role, company size and timeline fields to the discovery call form. Set up enhanced conversions, record booked calls as a secondary action, and create a "qualified call" stage in your CRM that uploads back to Google Ads as the primary conversion.

Why: Google learns from whatever you tell it is a success. Telling it a qualified call is success is the whole difference between leads and opportunities.

2

Week 2: Build three US Search campaigns

Campaign one: ClickUp implementation and consultant terms. Campaign two: migration terms from Asana, Monday and Jira. Campaign three: operations and workflow automation consulting, on a smaller test budget. Exact and phrase match only, US presence targeting, weekday business hours, and the full negative list live from the first day.

Why: Separate campaigns mean each intent gets its own budget, so a cheap but low quality cluster can never starve the one producing calls.

3

Week 3: Landing page and qualifying ad copy

Brief and review a dedicated implementation page. Write ads that state who the service is for, such as teams of 20 or more and structured implementation packages, so smaller teams self filter before they click.

Why: Every click that reads "not for us" in the ad and skips is budget saved for a buyer.

4

Week 4: Optimise on lead quality, not volume

Twice weekly search term reviews, budget moved toward the cluster producing qualified calls, and a weekly report showing qualified calls, booked meetings and cost per qualified call. Once qualified call volume allows, bidding moves to Maximise Conversions on that action only.

Why: Smart bidding needs clean, consistent signal. Switching it on before then wastes the first months of data.

Recommended starting budget
$3,000 a month for the first 90 days

Enough for three focused campaigns to gather clean data at US B2B click prices. Scale once cost per qualified call is known and stable, not before.
